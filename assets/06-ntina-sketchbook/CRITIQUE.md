# Ntina Sketchbook — every exploration, kept honestly

We learn, design and build intelligently, and we recognise what doesn't work so the next version can be exceptionally beautiful. Nothing here is thrown away. Each entry records the idea, what worked, what failed, the lesson, and what to try next.

Inspired by taiganja, the Kaili and Kulawi open-oval ornament of Central Sulawesi, and the Bomba flower of Donggala and Palu. Credit these origins whenever any of this work is shown.

Status key: **developing** (carry forward) · **parked** (has a lesson or a part worth reusing) · **retired** (kept only as a lesson).

## Round 2 — engraved metal (`02-engraved-metal/`)
Taiganja anatomy from the references (jonga horns + eyelets, tali ranga crab pincers, sopa), with chasing, granulation and filigree. Four metals: gold, brass, bronze, silver. Sheet: `ntina-engraved-sheet.png`.

| Option | Worked | Failed | Lesson | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Taiganja (engraved) | Faithful anatomy; convincing cast-metal look; metals as colourways | Too literal; simulated material fails as a reproducible mark (print, embroidery, small sizes) | Translate structure and meaning, never fake material (design-taste) | parked: useful for jewellery and foil or emboss production art |
| Bloom Within (engraved) | Rich detail rewards a close look | Same as above; too many parts | Reduce until it breaks, then add one thing back | parked |

## Round 3 — reproducible marks (`03-reproducible-marks/`)
Flat vector, one ink (plus an optional accent), traced from boolean masks. Tested one ink, reversed, at 48 and 24 px, and as a pattern. Board: `ntina-marks-board.png`.

| Option | Idea | Worked | Misreads or fails | Next improvement | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 · Cap | The mark as a batik cap: monoline copper strips | Prints and stamps in one ink; good pattern rhythm | Horn strips merge into one band; muddy at 24 px; reads as a pendant | Keep strips separate with even gaps; drop dot count for small sizes | developing (line version) |
| D2 · Void | Solid form, meaning in the cut-out | Strongest silhouette; holds at 24 px | Dot reads as an **eye** (bull face); notches read as **ears** | Its silhouette fed the Ntina mark | parked |
| D3 · Golden Construct | Spiral horns, 34-dot golden-angle flower | Elegant line; lovely flower | Horns read as **antennae** or **ram horns** | Its flower fed the Ntina mark | parked |
| D4 · Tile | Mark as a pattern unit; diamonds, rivers and blooms emerge at the joins | Most original pattern idea | Alone it reads as a **coffee bean**; corner blooms too faint | Rebuild around the Ntina form, top of the oval closed, stronger blooms | developing (pattern unit) |
| D5 · Ntina (synthesis) | Open-oval silhouette, 21-dot seed head in the opening, upswept horn crescent | Reads at a glance and rewards a closer look; no face reading; waves of horns in pattern | First version's curled horns read as a **moustache** (fixed) | Optical refinement: overshoot, horn weight, dot sizes at 24 px | developing (primary mark) |

## Round 4 — the Ntina system (`04-ntina-system/`)
Refined from D5, D1 and D4 using the design-taste skill. Inks: Bomba black, Bomba gold, Lagoon teal. Board: `ntina-system-board.png`. Now live in Batik Pattern Studio (Ntina stream, Ntina cascade, Ntina mark as hero).

| Part | Change | Misreading check | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary mark | Horns redrawn as one continuous elliptical U (no flat middle, no curls); body oval with slight overshoot; stem widens as it leaves the opening; 21-seed head | Moustache gone. At 16 px, horns over a circle edge towards the Taurus sign ♉ (also a bull, so the meaning fits); the open stem keeps it distinct | developing |
| Small optical size | Tried five large seeds: read as a **face / button with X eyes**. Final: empty opening, pure silhouette | Clean at 16-24 px | developing |
| Line version | Two contours, broken opening, 13 stamped dots, 8 seeds, horns as two separate parallel strips | Strips no longer merge; use from 48 px up | developing |
| Pattern unit | Mark at 0.56 scale; side half-diamonds make ketupat; corner quarter-blooms; a line of cecek carries each stem into the horns of the mark below | First horn draft drew as a dome (sign error), fixed | developing |

Lesson: build the system together (primary, small size, line, pattern) and test them side by side; optical sizes need their own drawing, not just scaling.

## What we have learned so far
1. Ornament grows from the construction; nothing floats.
2. Never fake a material in a mark; material belongs to production.
3. Every option gets a misreading check: eye, ears, moustache, antennae, coffee bean were all real catches.
4. Great marks come as systems: a primary mark, a line version and a pattern unit.
5. Ideas outlive drawings. Bloom Within's idea now lives in the Ntina seed head.
6. Optical sizes are separate drawings: what reads at 300 px can become a face at 24 px.
