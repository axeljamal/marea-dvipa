The case for direct

Everything the big platforms get wrong, fixed in one small house.

We stayed in enough rentals to know the pain points on both sides of the door. Here is what we changed.

The usual short-let experience

  • 15–20% in platform fees, split between you and the owner
  • A "cleaning fee" that appears at checkout and doubles the price
  • Twelve messages to find the Wi-Fi code and how the stove works
  • Generic listing, no idea what the street or the season is really like
  • Empty house all winter; sky-high rates for two summer weeks
  • No reason to come back, every booking is a stranger again
  • Owners juggling three calendars and praying for no double booking

What you get here

  • One all-in price. Linen, cleaning, heating, Wi-Fi, logs, and the hot tub heated for you. Nothing added at checkout.
  • Best-price promise. Always below the platform rate, with the comparison shown live.
  • A guest guide that answers itself. Search "stove", "bins" or "best swim" and get the answer.
  • Seasonal pricing that rewards quiet weeks. Gap nights, midweek fireside deals, work-from-the-nook rates.
  • Hotspot house tour. The story behind every object before you arrive.
  • Returning-guest club. 10% off direct, first dibs on Christmas and summer.
  • Owner dashboard. One calendar synced to every channel, direct share tracked.

The acquisition engine

Use the platforms for discovery. Own the relationship.

Airbnb and Booking.com are the shop window: millions of searches we cannot buy ourselves. The job of this site is to turn every platform guest into a direct guest the second time, and to be found first by everyone else.

Platform to direct funnel

  • Every platform guest gets a printed welcome card + QR to this guide, so the house itself sends them here
  • The guide shows a personal "next time, direct" code worth 10%
  • Post-stay email at day 2, anniversary email at 10 months, with their code
  • Listing title and photos on the platforms match this site exactly, so a search for the listing name lands here
  • Each channel gets its own code (BK10, AB10) so we see which converts

Be found first (SEO)

  • VacationRental schema in the page head, eligible for rich results and Google's vacation-rental panel
  • Google Business Profile as a holiday home with these photos and reviews
  • One landing page per intent: "dog-friendly cottage [town]", "cottage with wood-burner near beach", "workcation [county]"
  • Seasonal posts that answer real questions, each linking to the calendar
  • Fast page, real alt text, open-graph images for WhatsApp shares
  • 212 verified reviews republished here with schema, indexed on our own domain

Targeting that is actually allowed

  • Rate Reveal below: anyone comparing on a platform pastes the link and sees the direct saving instantly
  • Search ads on "[town] holiday cottage", UTM-tagged, only in shoulder months
  • Retargeting (with consent) for visitors who opened the calendar but did not book
  • Instagram and Pinterest: a reel per room, pinned to the tour
  • Partnerships: station café, yoga teacher, the pub. They hand out the QR, we send them guests
  • Scraping or messaging other platforms' users breaks their terms and GDPR. Everything above targets intent, not individuals.
