    Your arrival, step by step

    Before you travel

    We send a short house film and your door code 48 hours out. Tell us your arrival time and we will light the stove and warm the room.

    Getting here

    Train to the seafront station, then a 9-minute walk along the lane. The parking permit is in the hall drawer for the bay opposite.

    Self check-in from 3pm

    The key safe is behind the slate plaque. The code arrives by SMS. Lights and heating come on automatically when the door opens.

    During your stay

    Message us any time in the app. Median reply is 6 minutes. Extra logs are in the lean-to. Tide times are on the kitchen screen.

    Leaving by 10:30

    Leave the dishes, strip nothing. Late checkout to 1pm can be added if the next guests are not due.

    Accessibility statement · 8 November 2026

    Built to be used by everyone who stays here

    This site aims to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA. It is one page with no page builder, so what you see is what the browser gets.

    • A skip link, landmarks and headings in order, and every photograph described in text.
    • The whole site works with a keyboard: the calendar with the arrow keys and Page Up and Page Down, the dialogs with Escape, the tour and film with buttons that have names.
    • Text can be resized to 200% and the layout reflows on a phone without sideways scrolling.
    • Colours were checked for contrast in both the light and the dark theme, and the theme follows your system setting.
    • Nothing moves if your system asks for reduced motion; slideshows and the film have a pause control.
    • No cookies are set and nothing is tracked until you choose to allow it.

    Known limits. The house film has captions but no audio description. The interactive tour's floor plan is also offered as a list of rooms and stories for screen readers. The assistant's answers are plain text and can be read aloud by your screen reader.

    If anything here is hard to use, tell us by WhatsApp, phone or email and we will fix it or help you book another way. We review this statement each time the site changes.
